## What I did / built

- As a Break Through Tech AI Studio fellow, used an Allstate auto-claims case covering 188,318 claims to explore how an insurer could estimate final payout early enough for reserve planning, during the months repairs and legal outcomes take to settle.
- Validated the dataset, passing 18 data checks, and found average claim cost rose 64% from the low to the high range of one input field.
- Trained a random forest and an XGBoost model on a 70/15/15 split and compared them with simple cost baselines on 28,248 held-out claims. Average dollar error was $1,292 for the forest and $1,172 for XGBoost, versus $1,827 for guessing the typical claim cost.
- Training stopped after that first comparison.

- Collaborate in Git/GitHub with documented analysis methods and independent peer review so the work stays reproducible.
