## Look for

- Data science and analytics
- Machine learning and AI
- Applied AI, including LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation, and chatbots
- Cyber software engineering

SIEM, Elastic, and detection rules are a plus on those roles. They are not a reason to search for detection-engineering, SOC, or security-analyst internships.

Terms and schedule:

- **Spring 2027:** in class at VCU. Do not search for Spring 2027 internships

- **Fall 2027:** full-time co-op, with a lighter or paused course load

Locations:

- Remote
- Richmond, VA
- Northern Virginia, including Leesburg
- Washington, DC
- New York City
- Any other place only when the posting explicitly offers relocation assistance: housing, travel, or a relocation stipend

Pay:

- Paid internships and co-ops
- Funded fellowships (a stipend counts)
- Skip unpaid roles

Clearance and citizenship:

No industry is blocked. There is no target-company list. Search broadly.

## Skip

- Full-time jobs and new-grad roles
- Security analyst, SOC, detection-engineering, general software engineering, and IT roles
- Roles outside the list in "Look for"
- Postings that require a completed bachelor's degree
- Postings that require senior standing only, or graduation in 2026 or 2027
- Postings that require visa sponsorship now or later
- Locations outside the list above unless relocation assistance is explicit
- Unpaid roles, and roles that do not say whether they are paid
- Spring 2027 internships, and any part-time internship
- Postings whose application deadline has already passed
- A second copy of a posting that is already in [jobs.md](jobs.md) or [application-tracker.csv](application-tracker.csv)

## Skills that make a strong match

A posting does not need to name every skill. Treat it as a strong match when the work lines up with at least one of these:

- Python, pandas, and data analysis
- scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or model training
- LangChain, retrieval-augmented generation, or LLMs
- SQL
- Java
- SIEM, Elastic, or detection rules, when the role is still data, AI, or cyber software engineering

Git, Linux, and APIs are on the resume. They support a match. They are not enough by themselves.

## Where a listing goes

- **Matches:** the role, term, location, pay, and eligibility all fit. Pay must be stated.
- **Review:** the role and term fit, but one preference other than pay is missing or unclear. Say what is unresolved on **Why review:**. Do not use Review for a posting that never states pay.

When a listing is added, set **Application status:** to `Not applied` and add the same posting to [application-tracker.csv](application-tracker.csv).
